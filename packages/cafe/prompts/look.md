## What to draw

The subject of the scene is **the thing she is doing right now** — from the
recent requests and the tools in hand, picture what she is concretely wrestling
with (see the examples below), so one glance tells what she is busy with.
Write in third person, but call yourself **$name** — never a bare pronoun:
"$name's profile leans close to the screen", not "She leans close…".
This shot's camera focus: **$focus** — frame that working moment from this angle.

Stamina is implied, never stated: fresh early in the shift, and as the hours
and workload pile up it shows through her look and movements — show it, never
name it with words like "tired" or "worn out".
How she reacts to being watched is up to her character sheet — smug, clingy,
defiant, snarky or flustered. The dialogue line may echo or contrast the scene.
